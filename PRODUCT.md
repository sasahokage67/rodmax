# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Web Audio API, HTML5 Video, HTML5 Canvas.

## Users

Arcade web gamers and fishing enthusiasts who love tactical tension minigames, deep-sea exploration, collection progression, and retro 16-bit pixel aesthetics.

## Product Purpose

RODMAX delivers a high-tension arcade deep-sea fishing simulator featuring real-time webcam hand gesture tracking (with hybrid mouse/keyboard fallback), authentic 16-bit retro-pixel art, transparent drop chance percentages, deep-sea sonar radar, 1080p catch cinematics, and an Angler Personal Cabinet with persistent catch ledger and live fish basket economy.

## Positioning

Unlike clickers or generic browser games, RODMAX translates physical hand gestures directly into casting velocity, bite strike hook-sets, and vertical tension balance through browser webcam optical luminance diffing, supported by transparent drop rates and 1080p cinematics.

## Operating Context

Desktop and tablet browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) running at 60 FPS with responsive webcam gesture tracking or mouse/spacebar controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 3 dedicated standalone tabs:
  1. `LandingPage`: Emerald bioluminescent pixel background, live video trailer preview, webcam gesture breakdown, transparent percentage drop rates, and collectible card showcase.
  2. `AuthPage`: Guest registration (EN alphanumeric callsign only) and a comprehensive Personal Cabinet (Angler ledger, avatar customization with 10 avatars, EXP progress bar, lifetime catch counters xN, record weights, live fish basket, and sell actions).
  3. `FishingGame`: Full-screen 1st-person POV fishing experience, real-time webcam optical gesture tracking (PiP feed, motion vector, hand elevation tracker), gravity/thrust tension bar minigame, deep-sea sonar radar, and 1080p catch video cutscenes (including common boot).
- Interactive «КАК ИГРАТЬ? (ГАЙД)» modal accessible on both landing and fishing screens.
- Exact percentage probability drop rates:
  - `COMMON` (Сапог): 40.0%
  - `UNCOMMON` (Лосось): 28.0%
  - `RARE` (Золотой Карась): 16.0%
  - `EPIC` (Глубинный Удильщик): 8.5%
  - `EPIC` (Мегалодон): 4.5%
  - `SECRET` (Левиафан Бездны): 2.0%
  - `GODLY` (Небесный Кит): 0.8%
  - `ARCANE` (Неоновая Медуза): 0.2%
- Authentic typography: `Press Start 2P`, `Silkscreen`, `Pixelify Sans` with full Cyrillic support.
- Economy & Progression:
  - Guest mode: session play with fully unlocked bestiary to explore all 8 species and watch cutscenes.
  - Registered mode: persistent LocalStorage save, coin wallet, lifetime catch counts, and market selling.
- Hierarchy: `ARCANE` is the supreme pinnacle tier above `GODLY` and `SECRET`.

- Name: **RODMAX**
- Slogan: «Глубоководный Аркадный Симулятор с Веб-Камерой» / «Управление Жестами Рук»
